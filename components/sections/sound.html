Techno Babble Sound Bath

November 8, 2025 — Oracle Egg, BROILER Residency Series, Los Angeles, CA

A multimedia performance by Kai-Luen Liang, gamin, and Menghe Jing for voice clones, live voice, electronics, and traditional Chinese and Korean instruments. Framed as an imagined California sound bath—outer calm, often turbulent within—it explores how labeling and rumination shape perception. A real-time system captures video captions, voice dictation, and text-to-speech, then scrambles the language into sheets of sound. The performers improvise with these streams, turning cascading text into sound-poetry phrases, rhythmic stutters, moments of calm and noise.

Video & audio by Eli Klausner.

For Drifting Seas

Eating Music Label

The project For Drifting Seas is an exploration of "The South Manna Sea," an imaginary body of water near the Taiwan Strait. It uses sound as fictional world building for an area of water that is constantly flowing and connected to all its surrounding islands. Through sound composition, Kai-Luen explores water and islands as a metaphor for the constant changes in the everyday. How the sea moves is like the biological functioning of the body—never in a static state, always active, moving, changing even when it appears to be still. It is the ability to meet these changes that characterizes the individual's relation to the environment around it. These compositions are sonic stories for this imagined area of the "South Manna Sea."

The presentation of these stories is divided into two chapters: the digital EP contains 7 tracks—a general sonic overview, a mini sound world of this area, examining the individual in relation to the ground, the sky, the pulse of the heartbeat and ocean, the stars, the drift of the clouds, the concepts of home, and ultimately the ability to begin again in each moment. The cassette version contains 60 minutes of sound materials—a longer sound journey into the colors and details of the South Manna Sea, looking closer at the body and human voice as a vessel for carrying imagined memories.

《For Drifting Seas》是对"The South Manna Sea"概念的探索——位于台湾海峡附近的一个虚构水域，以声音构建的这片水系，它不断流动并与周围的各个岛屿产生关联。
