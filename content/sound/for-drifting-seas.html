The project [ For Drifting Seas ] is an exploration of "The South Manna Sea", an imaginary body of water near the Taiwan Strait. It uses sound as fictional world building for an area of water that is constantly flowing and connected to all its surrounding islands. Through sound composition, Kai-Luen is exploring water and islands as a metaphor for the constant changes in the everyday. How the sea moves is like the biological functioning of the body, never in a static state, always active, moving, changing even when it appears to be still. It is the ability to meet these changes that characterizes the individuals relations to the environment around it. These compositions are sonic stories for this imagined area of the "South Manna Sea."

Kai-Luen, who is a Los Angeles based musician and sound artist, brings a new concept album which aggregates years of exploration, cognition, and imagination of the world into sound waves, showing the fluidity of life itself, and constantly adjusting his perspective of self-observation between changes and invariance. This record serves as a document of these moments and changes in the form of sonic stories.

The digital version EP contains 7 tracks, which is a general sonic overview, a mini sound world of this area. It attempts to find a sense of belonging and ground amidst the constant drift and flow. It examines the individual in relation to the ground, the sky, the pulse of the heartbeat and ocean, the stars, the drift of the clouds, the concepts of home, and ultimately the ability to begin again in each moment.

The cassette version contains 60 minutes sound materials, which is a longer sound journey into the colors and details of the South Manna Sea. It looks closer at the body and human voice as a vessel for carrying imagined memories.
